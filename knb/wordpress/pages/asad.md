I am an astronomer and astrophysicist, an assistant professor and the director of the Center for Astronomy, Space Science and Astrophysics (CASSA) at Independent University, Bangladesh (IUB). I did a PhD in astronomy and astrophysics from [Kapteyn Astronomical Institute](https://www.rug.nl/research/kapteyn/?lang=en), University of Groningen, The Netherlands, and a postdoc at the [South African Radio Astronomy Observatory](https://www.sarao.ac.za/) before joining IUB in Jan 2020.

My research focuses on 21-cm cosmology, clusters of galaxies, radio astronomy antennas and machine learning in astronomy. Aside from research, I teach courses on astronomy and astrophysics and conduct outreach activities for the general public.

To reach me email or text at **kasad@iub.edu.bd **and check my calendar below.
