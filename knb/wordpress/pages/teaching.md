### Minor in Astronomy and Astrophysics

We offer a minor in astronomy and astrophysics open to all IUB students, but specially suited to the students of SETS. The minor emphasizes both theory and observation. There are 6 courses on observational astronomy and 4 courses on theoretical astrophysics. However, only 5 of these 10 courses will be offered in the first two years starting from Summer 2023. The five courses will be selected based on the capabilities, needs and demands of the students who enroll for the minor in 2023.

### AST 100: Our Cosmic (Hi)story

This course is offered for the students of IUB who do not have a math and science background. It is open to everyone, but specially suited for students coming from SBE, SLASS, SPPH and SELS. The course is specially popular among the students of SBE and SLASS. It is offered every semester and gives a general introduction to the whole universe based on the most recent discoveries in astronomy and astrophysics.

Students in this course take photos of deep-sky objects using a Unistellar eQuinox telescope. Details about the objects they observe are explained in the interactive lectures. Based on the photos and lectures, they have to prepare reports and give presentations as their midterm and final exams.
