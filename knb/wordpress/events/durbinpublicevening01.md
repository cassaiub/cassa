Celebrate a new chapter in stargazing with CASSA at our first-ever Durbin Public Evening, at Independent University, Bangladesh.This edition redefines our connection to the cosmos by blending immersive VR technology with high-powered telescope observations. The event bridges the gap between digital simulation and the raw beauty of the night sky, allowing you to experience the universe’s immense scale in virtual reality before witnessing its wonders firsthand through our lenses.

 

**Part 1: A virtual trip to space (6:30 - 6;50 PM)**

Everyone dreams of soaring through the stars, walking on distant planets, and witnessing the birth of a galaxy with their own eyes!

But let’s face it: unless you’re a billionaire or a professional astronaut, getting to space is a bit of a challenge. Most of us are stuck right here on solid ground, looking up and wondering "what if?"

But through the power of Virtual Reality, the entire universe is finally within your reach! You can stand in a digital planetarium where the ground disappears and constellations come to life, or zoom out from your own front door until the Earth is nothing but a tiny, glowing speck in the dark. You’ll learn to "read" the night sky and gain a perspective shift that changes how you see everything. Won’t it be fantastic to feel like the king of the cosmos without ever leaving the room?

**Part 2:Eyes on the Sky (7:00 PM – 9:****00 PM)**

**"Deep Space" View Via The Unistellar Equinox **:This is a smart telescope that "sees" in the dark. While traditional telescopes can sometimes show faint "gray smudges," the Equinox processes light to show you nebulae and galaxies in color, directly on a screen or eyepiece. You’ll see the swirling arms of galaxies millions of light-years away.

**Optical view via The Skywatcher 200P **:This is a classic, powerful "light bucket." It’s perfect for high-contrast, crisp views of the Moon’s craters, the rings of Saturn, or the cloud bands of Jupiter. It’s the raw, authentic experience of light hitting your eye from a planet millions of miles away.

 

**Flight simulator via Space Engine in VR** : While you wait your turn at the telescope, put the headset back on to "fly." Space Engine isn't just a map; it's a universe simulator. You can pick a star you see in the sky, fly to it in seconds, and land on a procedurally generated planet to see what the sunset looks like from another world.
