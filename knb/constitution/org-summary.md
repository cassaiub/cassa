# CASSA Organizational Structure — Summary

Source: **CASSA Constitution v2.0** (`CASSA_Constitution_v2.0.tex`). Clause numbers are v2.0. For research-specific framing see `research-spec.md`; for the people roster see `../people/roster.md`.

## What CASSA is
- An **autonomous, multidisciplinary research center of IUB**, dedicated to A&A and SPSE (cl. 1).
- **Administratively independent**, working directly under the **Office of the Vice Chancellor (VC)**; academic departments may hold **academic affiliation** with CASSA (cl. 2). IUB faculty members of CASSA are salaried by their home department (and teach its courses); their A&A/SPSE research belongs to CASSA (cl. 3.2).
- IP-manual precedence: where an IUB manual (Faculty/Staff/Purchase) conflicts with the constitution, the **IUB manual prevails** (cl. 6).
- Predecessors: **ARGI** (Astronomy Research Group, IUB; mid-2020) → **COALab** (Computational and Observational Astronomy Lab; late 2023) → CASSA (cl. 5).
- Approval: BOT 95th (1 Dec 2024) → AC 106th (9 Jul 2025) → Syndicate 104th (17 Jul 2025) → **VC Office Order 14 Aug 2025**. v1.2 adopted 14 Aug 2025; v2.0 current.

- "RA" = Research Assistant, "RI" = Research Intern (per organogram caption).

## Governance bodies & meetings
- **Directors** (cl. 11) — lead a division; term = tenure at IUB. Eligibility: PhD + ≥5 yrs postdoc/faculty experience in A&A/SPSE, established record.
- **Executive Director (ED)** (cl. 11) — rotates biennially between Division Directors (consecutive terms prohibited); oversees general administration, supervises the Manager (Science), facilitates cross-division decisions. Director-vacancy: ED is 30-day caretaker; successor by 2/3 vote of Directors+CMs+AMs; else new hire in 3–6 months (cl. 11.1).
- **Core Meetings** (cl. 12.1) — principal executive meetings. Directors + CMs vote (in person or email); **2/3 quorum** of sitting Directors+CMs. Decide fund disbursement, member status, substantive matters. Minutes by Manager (Science), circulated by ED, ratified within 3 days (cl. 12.2).
- **Scientific Advisory Board (SAB)** (cl. 18) — 3–5 external researchers (≥2 international), 3-yr terms, appointed by Core Meeting vote; annual review of research programme; written report to VC Office. External to IUB.
- **Dispute resolution** (cl. 8): ED mediation (14 days) → panel of 3 uninvolved CMs → VC Office (final); 30-day appeal right.
- **Conflict of interest** (cl. 9): mandatory declaration + recusal; ED keeps a conflicts register; if ED is conflicted, the other division's director chairs.
- **Discipline & removal** (cl. 19): 30-day written notice → Core Meeting discussion → **unanimous** vote (subject excluded). Director removals issued by VC; ED effects others on unanimous vote.
- **Hiring policy** (cl. 20, 11 sub-clauses): position creation (VC approval), advertisement (≥2 weeks), interview panel (≥2 members above the grade, ≥1 Director), selection, references (≥2), appointment via IUB HR, onboarding, **probation (3 months)**, renewal/non-renewal (30-day notice), EDI monitoring, failed-search review. Governs paid roles only.
- **Amendments** (cl. 7): by referendum in an inclusive meeting (or email) of all Directors + CMs + AMs.

- Each RA has a **primary + secondary supervisor**; supervision agreement at appointment; replacement within 30 days if primary leaves; RAs may raise welfare concerns directly with ED (cl. 15.1).

## Premises, assets & funds (Section C)
- **CASSA Office** = rooftop of the IUB main academic building, Bashundhara R/A, Dhaka (cl. 26); research workspace + workshops/mini-courses. Also houses the **BDOAA office**.
- All A&A/SPSE assets owned by IUB, managed by CASSA (cl. 21); ED is custodian; recorded in IUB Book of Accounts twice/year; audited annually (cl. 21.2, 25).
- **Funding sources** (cl. 27): (i) IUB annual budget, (ii) grants, (iii) mini-course/workshop revenue, (iv) other legal funding. Signatory authority: Directors, PIs, project leads only; Manager (Science) has none (cl. 27.5).
- **Annual report** by 14 August (cl. 27.3): grants, expenditure, CASSA-affiliated publications, teaching & outreach summary; published on the CASSA website.
- **Dissolution** (cl. 28): fixed assets revert to IUB; grants returned/transferred; student supervision moved to relevant IUB dept; Durbin transitioned/wound down; BDOAA EC assumes independent operation; requires VC Office Order.

## Vision & missions (Section D)
- **Vision** (cl. 29): "pathfinder in Bangladesh, impactful in South Asia, part of the international community" in A&A and SPSE.
- **Three missions** (cl. 30): **research, teaching, outreach** — each with an EDI framework following AAS norms (cl. 30.1).
  - **Research** (cl. 31): SITO; 9 action plans (see `research-spec.md`).
  - **Teaching** (cl. 32): support research components of the A&A minor; contribute to a proposed **MSc in A&A/SPSE** and the **DIASS track** (Data-Intensive Astronomy & Space Science) within MSc Data Science & AI; visiting instructors; summer/winter schools.
  - **Outreach** (cl. 33): **Durbin** (under DMT) and **BDOAA** (under its Executive Committee). See `../outreach/summary.md`.

### Code of Conduct (Appendix D) — headings
EDI (AAS norms); Research Integrity; Respect & Professional Conduct (anti-harassment); Professional Boundaries (power-differential relationships prohibited); Communication (official channels only); Physical Conduct (cultural-context dress; no alcohol/drugs/smoking on premises); Conflicts of Interest (→ cl. 9); Accountability (→ cl. 19 removal).

### Safeguarding (Appendix E) — headings
Zero-tolerance-to-inaction on SEAH; especially BDOAA, Durbin, school visits, rooftop nights. Roles: **CSFP** (Central Safeguarding Focal Point) and **PSFP** (Project Safeguarding Focal Point). Aligns with IUB policy, Children Act 2013, Prevention of Women & Children Repression Act 2000. Reporting to IUB Gender & Sexual Harassment Committee (safeplace@iub.edu.bd) and Proctorial Committee (proctor@iub.edu.bd). (Full standalone policy: `../outreach/CASSA Safeguarding Policy.md`.)
