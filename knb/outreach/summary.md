# CASSA Outreach — Content Spec

CASSA's **outreach mission** (Constitution cl. 33) runs through two national programmes — **Durbin** and **BDOAA** — both supervised by CASSA Directors and governed by their own teams under the **Outreach Manual (Appendix F)**. All outreach events are logged in the CASSA outreach log. A third pillar is the educational use of **IUB CORE** (rooftop observatory) for public astronomy nights.

Sources: `Durbin Manual.pdf`, `bdoaa.pdf` (BDOAA certification letter, 14 Aug 2025), `CASSA Safeguarding Policy.md`, and Constitution v2.0 (cl. 33, Appendices E & F).

## Durbin
**What it is:** A volunteer-based **astronomy outreach initiative** of CASSA — public engagement built around astrophotography, immersive (IR/VR) content, school visits, and "Astronomy Nights." Tagline in funding records: *"Durbin — Citizens of Distant Worlds."* ("Durbin" = Bangla for binoculars/telescope.)

**Origin & funding timeline:**
- **Founded 9 March 2023** by **Dr. Lamiya Mowla** (Wellesley College).
- Initial funding: seed grant from the **University of Toronto's Dunlap Institute for Astronomy & Astrophysics (UofT-DIAA)**, under an LOA (and amendment) between IUB and Univ. of Toronto — through **30 April 2026**. (Appendix B: 14,00,000 BDT, 2023, PI L A Mowla.)
- From **1 May 2026**: funded by a **British Council Bangladesh (BCB)** arts grant through **Women of the World (WOW)** — "Durbin — Women of the World," 19,59,400 BDT (Appendix B; PIs KMB Asad, FA Lima). Current manual covers **1 May 2026 – 30 Sep 2026**.
- Constitution: Durbin "shall always remain an outreach program conducted by volunteers under the supervision of the directors of CASSA" (cl. 33.1). Funding/affiliation changes are managed by the DMT in consultation with the Directors.

**Volunteer model:** Volunteers form **fixed teams** for a programme cycle (no transfers once constituted). Core tasks: (a) develop lasting materials — text, imagery, audiovisual, immersive — from Durbin astrophotography (2023–Jul 2026); (b) operate telescopes at Durbin & Astronomy Nights and run remote imaging camps; (c) produce physical prints / visual-art installations of deep-sky imagery. CASSA covers pre-authorized costs; completers get a **Letter of Experience**; winning teams at public exhibitions get cash prizes.

**Finance (key facts):** BCB provides 19,59,400 BDT to IUB, held in IUB's official bank account, spent per the 20 Mar 2026 approved budget. Advances drawn phase-by-phase from IUB Finance (new advance only after prior receipts cleared). DMT reimbursed against original receipts; volunteers via CASSA slips (no originals required — many live outside Dhaka). External partners funded via Letters of Agreement.

## BDOAA — Bangladesh Olympiad on Astronomy and Astrophysics
**What it is:** A **national educational outreach program** of CASSA (endorsed by a CASSA↔BDOAA certification/affiliation agreement, Ref iubcassa20250814a, 14 Aug 2025). The **BDOAA Office is at the CASSA Office** (Main Academic Building rooftop, IUB, Bashundhara R/A, Dhaka). National rounds funded by the **Dept. of Physical Sciences (DPS), IUB, since 2020**.

**Governance — three committees:**
- **Executive Committee (EC):** all CASSA faculty.
  - Certification letter & live site: **MA Momen — President**, **KMB Asad — General Secretary** (two-person EC).
  - Constitution Appendix F: **SA Uddin — President**, **KMB Asad — General Secretary**, **MA Momen — Chief Advisor**.
  - WARNING — **Discrepancy** to reconcile with the user (constitution vs. certification letter). The CASSA **Outreach Ambassador** (an IUB undergraduate) maintains communication between the EC and the two student committees.
- **Academic Committee (AC):** undergraduate & graduate students and young graduate professionals; owns academic content and evaluation.
- **Organizing Committee (OC):** undergraduate & graduate students; owns event logistics.

**Activities (three, per certification letter / Appendix F):**
1. **Olympiad** — regional rounds across Bangladesh -> national round; national K-12 winners represent Bangladesh at the **International Olympiad on Astronomy and Astrophysics (IOAA)** and **IOAA Junior**. CASSA is responsible for organising and funding the BDOAA team's IOAA participation each year.
2. **K-12 Workshop** — for school students, conducted by university students.
3. **K-12 Conference** — talks and poster sessions by and for school students.

## Observatories used for outreach
- **IUB CORE** (rooftop, IUB main academic building): educational observatory; outreach-night scheduling coordinated by the DMT (Durbin) or BDOAA EC. Portable optical telescopes (Ashvin 1/2 etc.) used for public sessions; visitors never operate instruments (a certified operator runs them). (Appendix G)
- **IUB Observatory** (Kaliakair): primarily research (0.5 m, OTA), not a public-outreach venue.

## Safeguarding (governs all outreach with minors)
CASSA maintains a **zero-tolerance-to-inaction** stance on abuse and SEAH, especially in BDOAA, Durbin, school visits, and rooftop astronomy nights (Constitution Appendix E; full standalone policy in `CASSA Safeguarding Policy.md`).
- **Scope:** all CASSA personnel, partners, students, contractors, guest speakers — physical or digital.
- **Roles:** **CSFP** (Central Safeguarding Focal Point — currently Research Affiliate **Rafia Rokaiya Hossain**) and per-programme **PSFP** (Project Safeguarding Focal Point).
- **Key rules:** >=2 CASSA reps at all night/rooftop events; never alone with a child in a closed/unlit space; activities in pairs/groups; photography of minors needs prior written parental + child consent; official CASSA/IUB channels only; vetting (reference checks + boundary interview) for those working with children.
- **Reporting:** to PSFP/CSFP, or directly to IUB Gender & Sexual Harassment Committee (safeplace@iub.edu.bd) / Proctorial Committee (proctor@iub.edu.bd). Survivor-centred; CSFP notifies IUB within 24 h for severe cases. CASSA defers to IUB bodies and Bangladeshi law (Children Act 2013, Prevention of Women & Children Repression Act 2000, Digital Security Act).
- Reviewed annually by Directors + Core/Associate Members.

## Quick facts for an Outreach page
- Two national programmes: **Durbin** (volunteer astronomy outreach, founded 2023 by L A Mowla) and **BDOAA** (national astronomy/astrophysics olympiad, DPS-funded since 2020, sends the IOAA team).
- Durbin funder transition: **UofT/Dunlap -> British Council Bangladesh / Women of the World** (from 1 May 2026).
- BDOAA feeds the **IOAA** and **IOAA Junior** international competitions.
- Both run under the **Outreach Manual (Appendix F)**; both supervised by CASSA Directors; both wound down/transferred on CASSA dissolution (cl. 28).
- Safeguarding is mandatory for all minor-facing activity (CSFP/PSFP structure).
