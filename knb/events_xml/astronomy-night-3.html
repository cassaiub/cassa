Regular Astronomy Nights (stargazing events) are beginning at IUB starting from this Thursday. You will get the chance to take images of the sky using a Unistellar eQuinox telescope and see the images on a large monitor at the Digital Classroom of IUB (BC2022). If more than half of the sky is covered in clouds, the images taken by eQuinox so far will be shown in BC2022 accompanied by an informal discussion and Q&A with an astronomer. The event is open to people of all ages from anywhere. However, please remember to RSVP before you come and also to check-in at Room BC2022 on time.
