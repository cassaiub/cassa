# Canonical CASSA facts (v2.0 — keep the site honest)

Reference for the **cassa-create** skill. Read this when authoring About / Research / org / People copy. The Constitution v2.0 is the source of truth; flag discrepancies, never fabricate.

- **Identity (cl. 1):** an autonomous, multidisciplinary research center of IUB
  for astronomy & astrophysics (A&A) and space & planetary science &
  engineering. Independent, under the VC Office (cl. 2). **Acronym flag:** the
  live site writes **"SPaSE"**, the constitution digest **"SPSE"** (same words) —
  constitution wins, but surface the discrepancy; don't silently swap.
- **Founded 14 Aug 2025** by VC Office Order; founder-driven by **K.M.B. Asad**
  (joined IUB 7 Jan 2020 → started ARGI). Lineage: **ARGI** (Astronomy Research
  Group, IUB; 2020) → **COALab** (Computational & Observational Astronomy Lab;
  7 Dec 2023) → **CASSA** (P&D 11 Nov 2024 → BOT 1 Dec 2024 → Academic Council
  9 Jul 2025 → Syndicate 17 Jul 2025 → VC Order 14 Aug 2025). Old asset domains:
  argiub.space, coalab.space. The full **genesis timeline** lives in the About
  page — **XML-only** (the .md scrape dropped it); read it from
  `knb/cassa.WordPress.2026-05-28.xml` when building About/history.
- **Research = five un-grouped research areas — NO DIVISIONS.** CASSA decided
  against divisions; the former OTA/RSS split was removed site-wide on
  **2026-05-30** (the `division` field, the `/research/ota` + `/research/rss`
  pages, the nav "Divisions" group, and all OTA/RSS pills/labels are gone). Do
  not reintroduce them. The five areas, all directly under CASSA, are:
  - **CHronOS** — Cosmic Hydrogen Observation and Simulation; the 21-cm sky,
    cosmic dawn & EoR.
  - **GATE** — Galaxies And Their Environments; galaxies, clusters & the ICM
    (radio + X-ray).
  - **RAIN** — Radio Astronomy Instrumentation; receivers, arrays & pipelines
    (incl. the STAR Telescope). **MATRiX is retired (→ RAIN).**
  - **Supernova** — Type Ia supernovae, the distance scale & cosmic expansion.
  - **Transient** — time-domain surveys & rapid follow-up.
  Presented on one `/research` page. Predecessors: **ARGI** (mid-2020) →
  **COALab** (late 2023) → **CASSA** (cl. 5).
- **Research mission = SITO** (Simulation, Instrumentation, Theory, Observation),
  cl. 31, with 9 action plans.
- **Infrastructure:** HPC **Timaeus** (Supermicro SuperServer SYS-740A-T: 2×
  Xeon Silver 4314 = 32c/64t, NVIDIA Quadro RTX A4000 16 GB, 192 GB RAM, 2 TB
  SSD, 24 TB HDD; bought via IBSS; arrived 25 Sep 2023); **IUB CORE** (rooftop edu observatory: optical scopes + the STAR Telescope);
  **IUB Observatory** (Kaliakair, 0.5 m optical); **STAR Telescope**
  (Small Transient Array Radio Telescope, formerly START; a CASSA build of the open-source TART = Transient Array Radio Telescope, by Shoaib Mirza). Route: `/research/star`. **Naming:** write "STAR Telescope" (or "START", or "Small TART") — never bare "STAR", which expands to only "Small Transient Array Radio".
- **Governance:** **two Directors — K.M.B. Asad and S.A. Uddin** (no "Division
  Director"); the **"Executive Director" title is removed site-wide for now** (do not add it) · Core/Associate/Graduate
  members · Affiliates · RAs/RIs · Scientific Advisory Board (cl. 18) · Core Meetings.
- **Three missions (cl. 30):** research (SITO); teaching (**Minor in A&A** —
  15-credit, UGC-approved, core AST 201 + AST 301, began 28 May 2023; plus the
  AST 100 "Our Cosmic (Hi)story" gen-ed course); outreach — **Durbin**
  (astrophotography & sci-comm, founded by Dr. Lamiya Mowla with Dunlap
  Institute support, inaugurated 9 Mar 2023) and **BDOAA** (Bangladesh Olympiad
  on Astronomy & Astrophysics → IOAA).
