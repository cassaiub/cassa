**GAZE** — the *General Astronomy Zoo Environment* — is a web-based annotation platform in the spirit of Galaxy Zoo, built to produce the labelled training sets that CASSA's machine-learning projects depend on. Through it, contributors mark up **radio AGN** in survey imagery and the **intracluster medium (ICM)** of their host clusters in *Chandra* X-ray images.

By turning expert and citizen inspection into clean, structured labels, GAZE feeds the [RGC](/research/projects/rgc-1) radio-galaxy classifier and the [MIMIC](/research/projects/mimic) cluster-mapping pipeline — closing the loop between human annotation and automated classification.
