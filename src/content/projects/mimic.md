**MIMIC** — *Mapping the Intracluster Medium in Chandra* — is an X-ray pipeline that turns archival **Chandra X-ray Observatory** data into maps of the hot **intracluster medium (ICM)** and hunts for the structures written into it: **radio minihalos**, **cold fronts**, shocks, relics and other edges.

The pipeline builds on tooling first written at CASSA (available on GitHub) for downloading and processing galaxy-cluster images from the *Chandra* archive, and extends it into full ICM mapping with automated front- and edge-detection.

The science motivation reaches back to earlier work on the possible connection between cold fronts and the radio-emitting minihalos of relaxed, massive clusters — cross-checking existing minihalo catalogues against the thermal structure seen in X-rays, and complementing recent MeerKAT radio images of the same systems.
