**RGC-2** is the ongoing successor to [RGC-1](/research/projects/rgc-1). Where the first model was trained on the 2,060-source FIRST-2060 set, RGC-2 scales to a **much larger labelled dataset** and moves to a **multimodal architecture** — combining the radio imagery with complementary information (source photometry, spectra and catalogue metadata) rather than pixels alone — to push accuracy and generalisation well beyond the first release.

The goal is a classifier robust enough to run across full radio surveys and to extend cleanly to new morphological classes, providing the environment-sensitive labels that downstream cluster science needs. Its training sets are curated through CASSA's [GAZE](/research/projects/gaze) annotation platform.
