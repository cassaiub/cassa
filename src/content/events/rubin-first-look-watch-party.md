🌌 You're Invited: Rubin First Look Watch Party at CASSA! 🌌

Join us at CASSA, IUB, for a truly historic moment in astronomy! 🚀

📅 Date: Monday, 23 June 2025
🕚 Time: 8:00 PM BST (Live stream starts at 8:30 PM)
📍 Venue: CASSA, Independent University, Bangladesh

We are thrilled to host a local Watch Party for the Rubin First Look — the global unveiling of the very first images from the Vera C. Rubin Observatory! 🌠 This marks the dawn of a new era in astronomy, and you can witness it live with the global science community.

🔭 What's happening:

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 08:00 | Guests arrive at CASSA office and Dr. Asad introduces the program |
| 08:15 | "Bringing the Night Sky to Life" — an interactive (with Q&A) introduction (with slides) to the Rubin observatory |
| 08:45 | A demonstration of the small telescopes of Durbin at CASSA office |
| 09:00 | Rubin First Look Unveiling Event livestream begins |
| 10:30 | Q&A about the livestream and about the future of Rubin |

All are welcome — students, astronomy enthusiasts, faculty, and the curious alike!

📡 Live stream in English (Spanish version also available)

Don't miss your chance to be part of this celestial milestone!
