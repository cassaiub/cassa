On the night of the **last full moon of 2025**, join us for a special screening of *E.T. the Extra-Terrestrial* (1982), directed by Steven Spielberg. Before the screening, an astronomer will offer a short introduction on the film's enduring significance in the **age of exoplanets**. In the early 1980s, astronomers worldwide were searching the Milky Way for planets around other stars, but no confirmed exoplanet would be discovered until **1992**, a full decade after *E.T.*. With more than **7,000** exoplanets known today, we can now revisit *E.T.* in the gentle light of the full moon, with a renewed sense of wonder and context.

After the **15-minute introduction**, the screening will begin at **6:15 PM** inside the CASSA Office. At the same time, just outside the office, you will also be able to observe the **full moon** through CASSA's telescopes, as well as with the naked eye.
