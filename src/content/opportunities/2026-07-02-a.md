The **Center for Astronomy, Space Science and Astrophysics (CASSA)** is recruiting **three Research Assistants (RAs)** to take on three distinct tasks over an **11-month term, 1 August 2026 – 30 June 2027**.

Each RA works under the supervision of a CASSA **Core Member** to conduct research and contribute toward publications. Applicants may be **post-baccalaureate or graduate** degree holders, and the **pay scale is determined by qualifications**. Exceptional candidates who do not meet the post-baccalaureate/graduate criteria will also be considered.

Note that these are **full-time positions** — 9 am to 5 pm, five days a week, **in-person at CASSA** — and an RA must not hold any other job or position during the term. **Currently enrolled students, undergraduate or graduate, are therefore not eligible.**

These positions are funded by the **CASSA Annual Budget**, and appointees will hold the official title **"CASSA Research Assistant."** Separately, we aim to open another circular in a couple of months for **"ARC Research Assistant"** positions — funded by the various **ARC (Accelerating Research at CASSA)** funds of our Core Members.

Before applying, **explore this website to understand CASSA's [research areas](https://cassa.bd/research) and the projects of our [Core Members](https://cassa.bd/people/core-members)** — your application should make clear which areas and people your skills and interests align with.

## The three positions

### RA (Astrophysics)

A solid background in astrophysics, demonstrated by relevant **coursework and papers**, and the ability to understand the science behind the data. This RA works closely with a Core Member on the scientific interpretation that turns observations and analysis into results.

### RA (Observatory)

Joins the group dedicated to **building the next observatories of CASSA** — with a particular focus on the **optical setup and its installation**. Hands-on, instrumentation-facing work assembling and commissioning the hardware that CASSA's future observations depend on.

### RA (Software)

Leads the **software pipelines** for analyzing data and **controlling the observatory**, along with other software needed across CASSA's research projects. The technical backbone connecting the instruments, the data, and the science.

## General responsibilities

- Conduct research under the supervision of a CASSA Core Member.
- Contribute toward publications.
- Work collaboratively across the astrophysics, observatory and software efforts.

## Terms

- **Positions:** 3 (one Astrophysics, one Observatory, one Software).
- **Term:** 11 months — 1 August 2026 to 30 June 2027.
- **Schedule:** Full-time — 9 am to 5 pm, 5 days/week — in-person at CASSA.
- **Eligibility:** Post-baccalaureate or graduate degree holders; exceptional candidates outside these criteria will also be considered. An RA must not hold any other job or position, so currently enrolled students (undergraduate or graduate) are **not eligible**.
- **Remuneration:** Scale determined by qualifications.
- **Official title:** CASSA Research Assistant (funded by the CASSA Annual Budget).
- **Deadline:** Apply by 28 July 2026 (extended from the original 16 July 2026).

Use the **Apply now** button on this page to open the official application form. Have your résumé, degree certificate and transcript ready as PDF files; the statements are written directly in the form, within the stated word limits.
