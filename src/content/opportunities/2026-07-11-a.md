The **Center for Astronomy, Space Science and Astrophysics (CASSA)** is recruiting **three Students on Duty (SODs)** — IUB's paid on-campus positions for currently enrolled undergraduate students — for the **Autumn 2026 semester, 1 August – 30 November 2026**: one **Outreach Ambassador** and two **Undergraduate Research Assistants (UGRAs)**.

Each SOD has a distinct individual role, described below. But the three also share one founding mandate: **together, they must establish an "Astronomy and Space Club" at IUB under CASSA** — the student club that will carry CASSA's activities into the wider IUB student body. Over the term, the three SODs will work as a team to organize the club, recruit its founding members, and set up its regular activities in coordination with the Office of the Director.

## The three positions

### Outreach Ambassador

Open to undergraduate students from **every school of IUB**, regardless of major. The Outreach Ambassador is responsible for **managing CASSA's outreach events** — Astronomy Nights, school programs and campus events — and for **liaising with BDOAA** (Bangladesh Olympiad on Astronomy and Astrophysics), CASSA's primary external outreach partner. Prior experience in event organization, teamwork, leadership or science outreach is a strong advantage.

### UGRA with Dr Syed Ashraf Uddin

Open to **SETS students only**. This UGRA joins [Dr Syed Ashraf Uddin](https://cassa.bd/people/uddin) in **supernova cosmology** — using Type Ia supernovae as standard candles to measure cosmic distances, chart the expansion of the Universe and probe dark energy and the Hubble tension, with particular attention to how a supernova's host galaxy biases its brightness. Expect hands-on work with supernova data and Python-based analysis under Dr Uddin's supervision.

### UGRA with Dr Khan Muhammad Bin Asad

Open to **SETS students only**. This UGRA joins [Dr Khan Muhammad Bin Asad](https://cassa.bd/people/asad) in **radio astronomy** — probing the cosmic dawn and the Epoch of Reionization through the redshifted 21-cm signal with LOFAR, and imaging the diffuse radio halos and mini-halos of galaxy clusters with MeerKAT. Expect hands-on work with radio-interferometric data and Python pipelines under Dr Asad's supervision.

## General responsibilities

- Carry out the duties of your specific role under the supervision of a CASSA Core Member or the Office of the Director.
- Work with the other two SODs to establish the Astronomy and Space Club at IUB under CASSA.
- Work in-person at CASSA.

## Terms

- **Positions:** 3 (one Outreach Ambassador, two Undergraduate Research Assistants).
- **Term:** 4 months — 1 August to 30 November 2026 (Autumn 2026 semester).
- **Eligibility:** Currently enrolled IUB undergraduate students. The Outreach Ambassador position is open to every school; the two UGRA positions are open to **SETS** students only (majors in CSE, EEE, ETE, CEN, CSC, Mathematics or Physics). You must **not already hold an SOD position anywhere else at IUB** — a student may hold only one SOD post at a time.
- **Remuneration:** 12,000 BDT per month.
- **Referee:** One IUB faculty member (Assistant Professor or above) who can comment on your suitability — contacted only if you are shortlisted, so get their permission in advance.
- **Deadline:** Apply by 20 July 2026, 11:59 pm.

## How to apply

Use the **Apply now** button on this page to open the official application form (sign in with your IUB email). Have your latest **transcript** ready as a PDF, downloaded from [IRAS](https://iras.iub.edu.bd); the statements on your relevant courses and experience are written directly in the form, within the stated word limits.

Apply for **one position per submission** — to apply for more than one, submit separately for each. After you submit, we email you a copy of your responses with a private link to review or edit them any time until the form closes.

For inquiries, write to [cassa@iub.edu.bd](mailto:cassa@iub.edu.bd).

*CASSA is an equal opportunity employer and encourages applications from diverse backgrounds.*
