The **Center for Astronomy, Space Science and Astrophysics (CASSA)** is seeking a highly motivated and talented post-baccalaureate student to join our research team as a **Research Assistant (RA)**. This position offers a unique opportunity to contribute to cutting-edge research in supernova cosmology, focusing on the expansion history of the Universe, dark energy, and the Hubble-Lemaître constant ($H_0$) tension.

- **Role:** Research Assistant (Post-Baccalaureate)

### Research Context

Type Ia Supernovae (SNe Ia) are critical "standardizable candles" used to measure cosmic expansion. While they led to the discovery of cosmic acceleration and the existence of dark energy, recent analyses have sparked debates regarding the nature of dark energy. Furthermore, a significant $5\sigma$ mismatch exists between measurements of $H_0$ from the early and late Universe. This research position aims to address these fundamental issues through astrophysical systematics.

### Key Projects & Responsibilities

The successful candidate will work on one or more of the following studies:

1. **Host Galaxy Correlation:** Investigating the effect of host galaxy age on the luminosity of SNe Ia.
2. **Local Environment Analysis:** Analyzing the impact of the local environment on SN Ia luminosity.
3. **Calibration Comparisons:** Comparing SN Ia luminosity calibration across various light-curve fitters.
4. **Standard Candle Improvement:** Improving SN Ia standardization through the analysis of host galaxy spectra.
5. **Pipeline Development:** Building a robust computational pipeline for the spectral analysis of SNe Ia.
