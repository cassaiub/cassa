The **Center for Astronomy, Space Science and Astrophysics (CASSA)** is recruiting **three Research Assistants (RAs)** to take on three distinct tasks over a **one-year term, 1 July 2026 – 30 June 2027**.

Each RA works under the supervision of a CASSA **Core Member** to conduct research and contribute toward publications. Applicants may be **post-baccalaureate or graduate** students, and the **pay scale is determined by qualifications**. Exceptional candidates who do not meet the post-baccalaureate/graduate criteria will also be considered.

Before applying, **explore this website to understand CASSA's [research areas](https://cassa.bd/research) and the projects of our [Core Members](https://cassa.bd/people/core-members)** — your application should make clear which areas and people your skills and interests align with.

## The three positions

### RA (Astrophysics)

A solid background in astrophysics, demonstrated by relevant **coursework and papers**, and the ability to understand the science behind the data. This RA works closely with a Core Member on the scientific interpretation that turns observations and analysis into results.

### RA (Observatory)

Joins the group dedicated to **building the next observatories of CASSA** — with a particular focus on the **optical setup and its installation**. Hands-on, instrumentation-facing work assembling and commissioning the hardware that CASSA's future observations depend on.

### RA (Software)

Leads the **software pipelines** for analyzing data and **controlling the observatory**, along with other software needed across CASSA's research projects. The technical backbone connecting the instruments, the data, and the science.

## General responsibilities

- Conduct research under the supervision of a CASSA Core Member.
- Contribute toward publications.
- Work collaboratively across the astrophysics, observatory and software efforts.

## Terms

- **Positions:** 3 (one Astrophysics, one Observatory, one Software).
- **Term:** One year — 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.
- **Eligibility:** Post-baccalaureate or graduate students; exceptional candidates outside these criteria will also be considered.
- **Remuneration:** Scale determined by qualifications.
- **Deadline:** Apply by 16 July 2026.

Use the **Apply now** button on this page to open the official application form. Have your résumé, degree certificate and transcript ready as PDF files; the statements are written directly in the form, within the stated word limits.

For inquiries, write to [cassa@iub.edu.bd](mailto:cassa@iub.edu.bd).
